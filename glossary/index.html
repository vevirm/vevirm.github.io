Example: dual-use

A technology or piece of research that can serve civilian purposes and military or security purposes. In this Radar, the important question is whether security demand changes funding, collaboration, access or control in European R&I.

Definitions are intentionally plain English. They explain how the term is used in this Radar, not every legal or academic nuance.
