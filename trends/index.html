How a pattern gets on this page

The weighting keeps the balance lively. The admission rule stays simple.

WHAT: Each side needs three current records from at least two sources.

WHY: One event or one publisher cannot make a trend.

WHAT: Stronger and more independent evidence gets more pull in the balance.

WHY: The number is a tug-of-war score, not a forecast.

WHAT: Older publications can show roots, persistence or reversal.

WHY: Material older than six months cannot create a current trend.
