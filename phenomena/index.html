What this page is trying to catch

Recurrence is the baseline. The interesting part is the shape of the recurrence.

LOOK FOR CHANGE: Which older words are giving way to different current words inside the same issue?

A topic can persist while its framing changes completely.

LOOK BETWEEN BOXES: Which two topics repeatedly arrive together, especially when that coupling is getting stronger?

The intersection can matter more than either category on its own.

ARGUE AGAINST IT: Every proposed pattern carries its most likely artefact. Attention score ranks what to inspect; it is not a probability.

The page should show how it could be fooled, not only why a pattern looks interesting.
